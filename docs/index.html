Privacy Policy

Job Application Tracker (Chrome extension) — last updated 10 September 2026

The short version: this extension collects nothing. Your data is stored on your own computer, is never transmitted anywhere, and nobody — including the developer — can see it. It asks for no site access unless you switch on automatic detection, which is off by default.

What is collected

Nothing is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared. The extension has no server, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party services, and no account system. No data ever leaves your browser.

What is stored, and where

When you click Save this page, the extension stores the company name, job title, page URL, the date, and the status you choose. This is written to chrome.storage.local, Chrome's local storage area on your own device. It is not synced across devices and is not readable by any website or by the developer.

Permissions

Granted on install, and these never change:

  • storage — to keep your list on this device.
  • activeTab — to read the title and URL of the tab you are viewing, only at the moment you click the extension icon.
  • scripting — to run a small reader on that page to pick out the company and role.

A default installation requests no access to any website. The extension cannot read a page unless you click its icon while on that page.

Automatic detection (optional, off by default)

You can switch on Detect applications automatically, which notices when you have submitted an application so you do not have to save it by hand. This needs standing access to job sites, so it is an optional permission that is off until you turn it on. When you do, Chrome shows you its own permission prompt naming the sites, and you can decline.

The sites it covers are LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday.

While it is on, a script runs on those sites and watches for a submission confirmation. It looks only for that confirmation and, when it finds one, reads the same job details described above. It does not read your resume, your messages, your contact details, form fields, or anything else on the page, and it still sends nothing anywhere — detected applications are written to the same local storage.

Switching the feature off revokes the site access as well as stopping the script. You can also revoke it yourself at any time from chrome://extensions, and Chrome will show it as a permission the extension is no longer using.

Deleting your data

Remove any entry with the ✕ button, or uninstall the extension to delete everything at once. Because the data exists only on your device, uninstalling is a complete deletion — there is no copy to request the removal of. Use Export CSV first if you want to keep a backup.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that would involve collecting data would also require a new permission, which Chrome shows you before it takes effect.
